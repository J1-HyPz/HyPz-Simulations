What these numbers mean

The model clears both bars the design set for shipping: it beats the base rate on every proper scoring rule, and it beats “the home team wins” on accuracy by nine points. Calibration is the part that matters most for using the output — expected calibration error means that when it says 30%, it happens close to 30% of the time.

It does not beat the closing line, and was never expected to. The gap is small and stable at roughly 0.01–0.02 Brier per season.

One caveat worth knowing. Accuracy barely discriminates here. The model names a draw as the single likeliest result times in , because a draw's probability tops out near 0.37 and a favoured side's exceeds that. Its top pick is therefore 99.5% identical to “the stronger team wins”. Judge it on Brier, log loss and calibration, not on how often it calls the winner.
