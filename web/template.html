These forecasts are not yet validated

The parameters are sound — home advantage of and both sit where the literature puts them — and the model independently rated Wolves, Burnley and West Ham as the weakest sides in 2025/26, which are exactly the three relegated. That is encouraging, not evidence.

Nothing here has been tested against held-out data. A model can look entirely reasonable and still be beaten by “the home team wins”. Phase 2 is the walk-forward backtest — Brier score, log loss, a calibration curve, and comparison against three baselines including the market closing line, which football-data.co.uk ships in the same files. Until those numbers exist, treat this as a working slice rather than a forecast you would act on.
