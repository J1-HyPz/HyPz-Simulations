Accuracy

Measured, not asserted

What these numbers mean

It clears both bars set for shipping: it beats the base rate on every proper scoring rule, and beats “the home team wins” on accuracy by nine points. Calibration matters most for using the output — expected calibration error means that when it says 30%, it happens close to 30% of the time.

It does not beat the closing line, and was never expected to. The gap is small and stable at roughly 0.01–0.02 Brier per season.

One caveat worth knowing. Accuracy barely discriminates here. A draw is named likeliest only times in , because a draw’s probability tops out near 0.37 and a favoured side’s exceeds it. The top pick is therefore 99.5% identical to “the stronger team wins”.
